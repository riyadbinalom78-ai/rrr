About Me

The Stranger in Search of Truth and Justice

Student • Explorer of Science, AI, Islam & History • Voice for the Oppresse.

I have always felt like a quiet observer in a world that never stops talking. While the crowd rushes toward the latest trends and fleeting validations, I find myself drifting in the opposite direction—toward the timeless, the profound, and the unseen. My journey is not defined by the company I keep, but by the weight of the questions I ask and the truths I seek within the intersections of faith and reason.

For me, Islam is the anchor that prevents my soul from being swept away by the chaos of modern society. It is a philosophy of peace, a manual for discipline, and a constant reminder that my purpose transcends this temporary existence. When I look at the universe through the lens of Science, I don't just see equations; I see the intricate design of a Creator. When I look back at History, I see the rise and fall of civilizations that remind me of our own fragility and the weight of our legacy.

However, my search for truth is not just theoretical; it demands action. I cannot remain a silent observer when humanity is tested. I stand firmly in unwavering solidarity with Palestine. I believe that freedom is a God-given right, not a political privilege. I have taken to the streets and raised my voice in protest, joining the global movement for their liberation, because I know that silence in the face of oppression is complicity.

I embrace the identity of a "Stranger" in these times. In this age of digital noise, choosing to stand for unpopular truths often leads to a certain kind of solitude—a loneliness that I have grown to cherish. It is in this isolation that I learn to unlearn the superficialities of the world. I strive to grow step by step, with patience and curiosity, seeking a balance between a sharp mind, a humble heart, and a courageous spirit.

My goal is simple yet demanding: to become a mindful individual who contributes to the world not just with information, but with insight and justice. I am a student of the eternal, a traveler through time, and a believer in the beauty of being misunderstood by a world that has forgotten how to reflect.

— Unmasked. Authentic. Resilient.

“In a world of convenient silences, I choose the uncomfortable truth: Solidarity with the resilient soul of Palestine.🇵🇸”
